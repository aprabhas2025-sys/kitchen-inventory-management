What We Offer

Everything your kitchen
needs to thrive

A complete system built for the demanding pace of professional kitchens — from a single bistro to multi-location restaurant groups.

🥬

Ingredient Tracking

Track every item in real-time — quantity, unit, location, expiry date, and cost per unit. Full granular visibility across your entire pantry.

🤝

Supplier Management

Manage all your supplier contacts, pricing tiers, lead times, and reliability scores in one place. Never miss a delivery window again.

📋

Purchase Orders

Generate, send, and track purchase orders automatically. Receive confirmations, log deliveries, and reconcile invoices seamlessly.

📊

Usage Logging

Log ingredient usage per service, per dish, or per shift. Understand your consumption patterns and eliminate costly waste.

🔔

Low Stock Alerts

Set intelligent thresholds and receive instant alerts via email or SMS when stock drops below your service requirements. Never 86 a dish again.

📈

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize food cost trends, waste reduction metrics, and supplier performance over time. Make data-driven decisions that protect your margins.
